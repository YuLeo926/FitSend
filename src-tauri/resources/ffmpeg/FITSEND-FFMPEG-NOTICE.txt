FitSend video support uses the separate FFmpeg and FFprobe command-line programs.

The bundled Windows release uses FitSend's source-pinned FFmpeg 8.0.1-fitsend1 build. The precise source revisions are recorded in SOURCES.lock and BUILD_README.txt beside the executables. The applicable GNU GPL v3 license is included as LICENSE.GPLv3.txt; dependency copyright, license and patent notices are in THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt. This FFmpeg build is GPL v3 or later.

Corresponding source
--------------------
Download FitSend-FFmpeg-8.0.1-fitsend1-sources.tar.gz alongside the Windows packages at https://github.com/YuLeo926/FitSend/releases/tag/v0.3.0 . It includes the exact FFmpeg, x264, libvpx, Opus, zlib and dav1d source archives used, their SHA-256 hashes, build scripts, generated configuration, toolchain versions and license notices. Rebuild instructions are in materials/recipe/README.md inside that archive. No upstream media source patches are applied.

BUILD_PACKAGE_SHA256SUMS.txt records paths in the original FFmpeg build package (bin/ffmpeg.exe, bin/ffprobe.exe, LICENSE and README.txt). FitSend stages those files into this directory and renames LICENSE and README.txt to LICENSE.GPLv3.txt and BUILD_README.txt; executable bytes are unchanged.

Replacing the tools
-------------------
FitSend looks for ffmpeg.exe and ffprobe.exe in its bundled resources first, and then falls back to the operating-system PATH. Advanced users may override either executable with FITSEND_FFMPEG_PATH and FITSEND_FFPROBE_PATH.
